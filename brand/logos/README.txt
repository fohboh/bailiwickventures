BAILIWICK — LOGO KIT
Trajectory mark. Wordmark set in Source Serif 4; subline in Inter.
All text is CONVERTED TO OUTLINES. No fonts required to open, print or send these.

WHAT IS HERE

  mark-<color>                 The icon alone. Square, 64x64 artboard.
  landscape-<entity>-<color>   Icon left, name right. The default lockup.
  portrait-<entity>-<color>    Icon centred above the name. Stacked/vertical.

  entity   ventures | venture-studio | vibe
  color    navy | black | white

  svg/   Vector originals. Infinitely scalable. Use for print, signage,
         embroidery, laser, vinyl, and anything being resized.
  png/   Transparent background. Marks 1024 px, landscape 2048 px wide,
         portrait 1200 px wide. Use for slides, documents, and the web.

WHICH ONE TO USE

  Landscape   Default. Website headers, email signatures, letterhead,
              slide masters, business cards. Use it unless you cannot.
  Portrait    When the space is taller than it is wide — banners, tote
              bags, pop-up stands, social avatars with room beneath,
              a centred title slide.
  Mark alone  Favicons, app icons, avatars, watermarks, anywhere the
              name already appears next to it. Never smaller than 16 px.

COLOR

  navy   #12304F   Primary. What the website uses.
  black  #0B0C0E   One-color reproduction: print, fax, engraving, stamps,
                   embroidery, newspaper. Also correct on any pale ground.
  white  #FFFDF9   Reversed, for dark or photographic backgrounds.

  The mark is single-color by design and has no color dependency. If the
  brand color changes, only one value changes with it.

CLEAR SPACE AND MINIMUM SIZE

  Clear space: the diameter of the largest node on all four sides. Nothing
  intrudes — not type, not a rule, not an image edge.
  Minimum size: mark 16 px on screen, 6 mm in print.
  Landscape lockup: do not reproduce below 120 px / 30 mm wide.

WHAT NOT TO DO

  No gradients. No outlines or strokes added. No recoloring individual nodes.
  No stretching — scale proportionally, always. No rotation: the climb left
  to right is the entire idea. Do not straighten the branch; the branch is
  what keeps the mark from being generic. Do not rebuild the lockup by
  setting the type yourself — the spacing here is deliberate.

SENDING THESE TO A PRINTER OR FABRICATOR

  Send the SVG. Text is already outlined, so nothing can substitute a font
  or reflow. If they ask for EPS or PDF, any SVG opens in Illustrator and
  exports to both without loss.
